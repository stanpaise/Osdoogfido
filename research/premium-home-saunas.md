# Premium Home Saunas (Infrared & Traditional) — Niche Research

**Verdict:** #1 recommended affiliate niche, based on search demand, competition analysis, and affiliate program availability.

## Why This Niche Wins

### 1. High Search Volume (USA)
Search interest for "home sauna," "infrared sauna," "sauna benefits," and "sauna for home" is consistently strong year-round. Premium wellness niches show evergreen demand and rank among the top high-ticket categories in 2026.

### 2. Low Competition
Compared to other wellness niches, saunas have very few affiliate sites, especially in the $5k–$10k price range. Most competitors are manufacturers rather than SEO-optimized blogs, which leaves room to outrank them with:
- 20–30 well-written articles
- Comparison guides
- "Best sauna for…" content
- Installation guides

Premium wellness niches (saunas, ice baths) are specifically identified as low-competition and beginner-friendly.

### 3. Easy Affiliate Registration
Sauna brands typically approve beginners because they want more exposure, carry high margins, and rely heavily on affiliates. Programs are usually hosted on ShareASale, Impact, or CJ, making signup simple.

### 4. Beginner-Friendly Content
No technical expertise required. Content ideas that write themselves:
- "Infrared vs traditional sauna — which is better?"
- "Best home sauna for small spaces"
- "Sauna benefits backed by science"
- "Top 5 saunas for detox and recovery"

### 5. High-Ticket Products ($5k–$10k)
Most premium home saunas fall directly in the target commission range:
- Clearlight Sanctuary models
- Sunlighten Signature series
- JNH Lifestyles premium infrared saunas

| Metric | Value |
|---|---|
| Average sale | $6,000–$10,000 |
| Typical commission | $800–$2,000 per sale |

### 6. USA Demand Is Exploding
Targeting the USA works well from Canada because:
- The U.S. wellness market is massive
- Home renovation + wellness spending is rising
- Saunas are trending in fitness, longevity, and biohacking communities
