# Validation plan: prove roofers will pay before building more

The scanner is the validation tool. Don't add features until this plan says yes.

## Why roofing (the decision)
- **Money:** roofing has among the highest lead costs in home services: about $71–162 per Google Local Services lead in 2026 benchmarks, versus a ~$53 home-services average. Jobs run $10k+.
- **AI fits the purchase:** a roof is a researched, expensive decision, exactly when people ask an AI assistant for a recommendation.
- **The gap:** generic AI-visibility checkers are free or cheap (HubSpot, BrightLocal, Distribb; Otterly at $29/mo), and enterprise tools (Profound) sell to franchises at four to five figures a month. Nobody owns a self-serve tool *for a single roofing company*.
- **The risk:** marketing agencies already pitch "AI SEO" to contractors. You win by being cheaper, faster and roofing-specific, not by being smarter.

## The 14-day test

**Days 1–2: get 30 scans of real roofers**
- Pick 2–3 mid-size metros (storm-heavy markets like Dallas, Denver, Oklahoma City, Tampa).
- Scan 10 roofers per metro. Save each result (screenshot or PDF).

**Days 3–10: send the results cold**
- Email or DM the owner. Leads with the competitor line, not the score:
  > "I asked an AI assistant 5 questions homeowners ask about roofers in Dallas. It recommended Summit Peak Roofing in 4 of 5 answers. You weren't mentioned in any. Here's the full report and the 3 fixes. Want me to re-check monthly and track it against them?"
- Channels: email from their website, Facebook roofing-contractor groups, LinkedIn. Also r/Roofing (read the rules first; don't spam).

**Days 10–14: ask for money**
- Offer: **$49/mo** for monthly tracking across AI assistants plus a competitor alert. Founding price $29/mo for the first 10.
- Use a Stripe payment link. A "yes, sounds interesting" doesn't count; only a payment does.

## Go / no-go
| Result after 30 cold reports | Decision |
|---|---|
| 3+ paid | **Go.** Build monthly tracking, the ChatGPT/Perplexity checks and a PDF report. |
| 1–2 paid, several replies | Tweak the offer (price, a done-for-you fix package at $300–500) and run 30 more. |
| 0 paid, few replies | Kill it or switch verticals (try water damage restoration or med spas). Total cost so far: a few dollars of API calls. |

## What to build next (only after "Go")
1. Add ChatGPT, Perplexity and Gemini answers (customers ask for ChatGPT by name).
2. Saved reports and monthly re-scans with email alerts ("Ironclad Roofing overtook you").
3. Downloadable PDF report for owners to forward to their marketing person.
4. Stripe checkout and accounts.
